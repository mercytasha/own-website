Plan Your Visit

We're excited to welcome you — here's a quick guide to make your first visit comfortable and easy.

When We Meet

  • Sundays — 10:00 AM — Main Service (family-friendly)
  • Wednesdays — 6:30 PM — Midweek Prayer & Teaching

What To Expect

Casual dress, welcoming people, 45–60 minutes of worship and a practical, scripture-based message. Childcare and youth programs run during the main service.

Parking & Accessibility

Free on-site parking is available. The building is accessible and we offer seats close to the entrance if you need them — contact us in advance and we'll reserve space.
